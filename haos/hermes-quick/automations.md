Save under `$HERMES_HOME` not `~/`.

Weekdays 6am: 10 merch/print shops (San Diego + online). Name, site, one pain line. Save `$HERMES_HOME/leads/leads_DATE.csv`. Message top 5. Do not email them.

## 2 Content
Weekdays 8am: one Horns & Halos caption + 3 X lines. Direct, no fluff. Save `$HERMES_HOME/content/drafts/`. Do not post.

## 3 Outreach
Weekdays 2pm: from today's lead file, one 80-word draft each for the top 3. Save only.

## 4 Research
Daily 5am: three competitor moves in occult/fantasy merch + one product to feature. Save `$HERMES_HOME/reports/`.

## 5 Schedule wrap
Friday 4pm: list files created this week under `$HERMES_HOME`. Ten lines max.
